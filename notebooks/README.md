# Notebooks

The number prefix says what kind of notebook it is.

- 00 to 09: walkthroughs of the pipeline on real data, in pipeline order.
    - 00 eddy identification on one SWOT day
    - 01 eddy tracking and the track files
    - 02 PACE observations per eddy after collocation
    - 03 Rossby number and speed maps
- 10 to 19: checks of the SDP pigment retrieval.
    - 10 how SDP reads PACE spectra in the Gulf Stream region
- `archive/`: the early SDP pigment exploration from April to July 2026 (polarity, seasonality, DPA, random forest). They run on today's data, but their written conclusions came from SDP values from before the 2026-10-13 wavelength fix, and the paper now uses Copernicus CHL instead.

Paper figures live in `research_paper/`, poster notebooks in `conferences/`, and dated advisor updates in `research_updates/`.
