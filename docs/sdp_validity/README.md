# Wavelength truncation hides SDP's Sargasso failure

The SDP assumptions hold in one part of the region and fail in the rest. For eddies centered north of the Gulf Stream axis, from April to October, PACE spectra fall inside the range of the 145 training spectra and SDP Tchla follows independent chlorophyll (correlation 0.79 against PACE `chlor_a` over the 1,025 north-side eddy-composites of all months). On the Sargasso side the assumptions fail in every season: from March to August SDP Tchla runs a median 3 to 5 times independent chlorophyll and in clear summer water sits near 0.2 to 0.3 mg m^-3 whatever the chlorophyll is, and from September to January a seasonal feature at 687 to 688 nm in PACE Rrs pulls Tchla to zero in 12% to 41% of interior pixels. North of the axis from November to March, 27% to 81% of pixels lie outside the training range. One code defect changes every stored number: `src/eddy_tracking/pipeline/collocate_pace.py:151` truncates the PACE band centers to whole nanometers, which raises Tchla by a factor of 1.46 (ratio of means, 150 pixels) or 1.40 (median pixel ratio, 2,000 pixels) and hides about two thirds of the zero-clipped pixels. Fixing the truncation lowers Tchla and roughly triples the zero-clipped share, so the fix exposes the Sargasso-side limit; it does not remove it. Of the 13 pigments, Tchla and five pigments that follow it closely (Fuco, Chlc12, Chlc3, HexFuco, ButFuco) can support a relative biomass claim north of the axis from April to October. Zea, DVchla, Perid, MVchlb, Neo, Viola, and Allo cannot support a claim anywhere in the region yet, and no ratio to SDP Tchla is usable on the Sargasso side.

## How to read the evidence

- Five research notes dated 2026-10-13 in this folder (`docs/sdp_validity/`) supply the numbers. I call them the audit (`implementation_audit.md`), the envelope check (`training_envelope_check.md`), the method note (`sdp_method_assumptions.md`), the regional note (`gulf_stream_optics_and_pigments.md`), and the data-quality note (`pace_oci_rrs_quality.md`).
- "Measured" and "confirmed by running" mean a researcher ran a script on files on disk. "Inferred" means reasoning from those results with nothing run. "Recalled" means from memory and unchecked. Each label below is the researcher's own.
- The samples differ in size. The audit's real-pixel sample is 150 interior pixels from 6 eddies and 28 composites. The envelope check scored 11,563 interior pixels in 24 strata (calendar month by side), reran 2,000 of them with the true band centers, and counted all 1,249,453 interior pixels of the 3,571 gold composites (2024-03-08 to 2025-12-29) for zero and impossible outputs.
- "North side" and "south side" (the Sargasso side) are the side of the eddy center on that composite, from the sign of `gs_dist_km`. No note classifies single pixels by side.
- The measurements were taken between about 19:30 and 20:10 on 2026-10-13, while another session was extending the experiment in place. They use the record that ended 2025-12-29 (3,571 gold composites). The rebuilt gold pigment table has 4,343 rows to 2026-05-28, so the composites from January to May 2026 are in no count below, and the winter and spring months rest on one more year of data than this report used.
- Every stored pigment was produced with the truncated wavelengths, so every by-month and by-side number below carries the truncation unless it says "true centers".
- The envelope check writes concentrations in ug/L, which equals mg m^-3. This report uses mg m^-3.

## Where the assumptions hold, by water type and season

- North side (slope water and shelf-influenced water), April to October: the assumptions mostly hold for Tchla as a relative biomass index.
  - 3.0% to 11.9% of scored pixels lie beyond the 95th percentile of held-out training spectra, where 5% is expected (measured, 380 to 500 pixels per month).
  - SDP Tchla against PACE `chlor_a` on the north side, all months: correlation of the logs 0.79, median ratio 1.55, log-log slope 0.59 (measured, 1,025 composites, truncated wavelengths). The median ratio is 1.34 from March to May (301 composites), 1.91 from June to August (425), and 1.39 from September to November (248).
  - Tchla is clipped to zero in 2.0% or less of interior pixels in every month except December and January (measured).
  - The training support is thin. The method note counts 16 training samples inside 30 to 45 N, 80 to 50 W, all from July to September; the envelope check counts 14 inside 29 to 44 N, 81 to 56 W. Reading their temperature and salinity, the method note places none of them in the slope sea (inferred, medium confidence). No HPLC from this water in the PACE era has been compared with SDP.
- North side, November to March: the assumptions do not hold.
  - Share of pixels beyond the training 95th percentile: November 31.1%, December 80.9%, January 70.3%, February 38.8%, March 26.6% (measured, 380 to 500 pixels per cell; January rests on 8 composites and February on 14).
  - In January, 20.0% of north-side pixels have an inversion that drives chlorophyll to zero and assigns the absorption to colored dissolved matter (measured).
  - Only 2 of the 145 training samples come from north of 25 N between October and February (measured).
- The stream itself: not measured. The notes split pixels by the side of the eddy center, and the method note finds no training sample it can place in the Gulf Stream core (inferred, medium confidence). Eddies that cross the axis give the only indirect evidence: 5.8% of scored pixels in movement class NS (470 pixels) and 10.0% in class SN (558 pixels) lie beyond the training 95th percentile.
- Sargasso side, March to August: the spectra look in range and the outputs are still wrong.
  - 2.4% to 13.7% of pixels lie beyond the training 95th percentile, and Tchla is clipped in 0.5% to 4.0% of pixels (measured).
  - SDP Tchla runs a median 2.95 times `chlor_a` from March to May (792 composites) and 5.31 times from June to August (870 composites, 98.4% beyond a factor of 2) (measured).
  - In composites with `chlor_a` of 0.05 mg m^-3 or less, the correlation between SDP Tchla and `chlor_a` is 0.11 (752 composites); from 0.05 to 0.1 it is -0.09 (915 composites) (measured). The assumption that the residual derivative carries a chlorophyll signal fails at these concentrations.
- Sargasso side, September to January: the assumptions fail and the outputs show it.
  - Tchla clipped to zero: September 12.1%, October 22.1%, November 32.9%, December 41.3%, January 16.0% of south-side interior pixels (measured). In December 71.9% of south-side pixels fail at least one of three physical checks (zero Tchla, DVchla above Tchla, accessory sum above 1.64 times Tchla).

## Which pigments can still support a claim

This section is my synthesis of the notes. No note states it in this form.

- Tchla: a relative biomass index north of the axis from April to October, after the rerun with true band centers. Its absolute level is not usable anywhere yet, and it is not usable on the Sargasso side at all.
- Fuco, Chlc12, Chlc3, HexFuco, ButFuco: the same biomass claim as Tchla in the same water and months, and no separate composition claim.
  - Cross-validated R^2 in Kramer et al. (2022) is 0.54 to 0.70 for these five, but in the same training data a straight line on measured Tchla explains 0.62 to 0.87 of their variance (method note, computed from the PANGAEA file; the baseline is in-sample, so the comparison favors it).
  - On 901 eddy-days the SDP eddy means of these five track SDP Tchla at r^2 0.76 to 0.98 (project memory, L3 pipeline with truncated wavelengths).
  - Use the concentration. A ratio of any of them to SDP Tchla inherits the Tchla problems below.
- MVchlb, Neo, Viola, Allo: no claim. The paper's mean absolute error is 0.98 to 1.22 times the mean concentration and its cross-validated R^2 is 0.38 to 0.42. Allo and Neo are zero in 70% and 74% of the training samples. With true band centers these four are clipped to zero in 35.0% to 45.0% of pixels (measured, 2,000 pixels, weighted).
- Zea: no claim. SDP Zea correlates negatively with HPLC Zea in two local runs (log r -0.196 in the 332-cast PVST run; rank correlation -0.311 at CalCOFI, 27 stations; project memory). The regional note's explanation is that the Zea to Tchla relation changes sign between regimes (log-log slope +0.99 at BATS, -0.21 at CalCOFI), which is an inference with moderate confidence.
- DVchla: no claim until regional HPLC supports one, because two results conflict. In the PVST run DVchla was the one pigment where SDP beat a chlorophyll law (rank correlation 0.397 against -0.018). In this region SDP clips DVchla to zero in 23.7%, 29.3%, and 26.1% of interior pixels in June, July, and August, mostly on the Sargasso side, where Prochlorococcus should be most abundant (measured zeros; the biological reading is the envelope check's inference).
- Perid: no claim. It has no regional test, it is zero in 54% of the training samples, and in the paper modeled Perid clusters with the cyanobacterial pigments while measured Perid clusters with the diatom pigments.
- Pigment groups: the notes support one biomass signal. They do not support the five-group structure in this region.
  - Hyperspectral Rrs holds about four independent quantities (Cael et al. 2023), and the authors' later work separates three communities (Kramer et al. 2024).
  - In SDP eddy composites HexFuco/Tchla and Zea/Tchla correlate at r 0.94; in BATS HPLC the same two ratios correlate at -0.01 (358 samples) (regional note, from project memory). The regional note attributes the SDP value to the shared Tchla denominator (inferred), so a cluster that joins haptophyte and cyanobacterial pigments most likely comes from the method.

## Defects in the code and pipeline, largest effect first

1. Band centers are truncated to whole nanometers (confirmed).
   - Evidence: `src/eddy_tracking/pipeline/collocate_pace.py:151` applies `.astype(int)` to the wavelength coordinate and names the columns `Rrs_<int>`. `src/eddy_tracking/packages/sdp/prediction.py:259-262` rebuilds the wavelength vector from those names and passes it to the spline. The same truncation is at `src/eddy_tracking/pipeline/background.py:123` and `sdp-validation/sdpval/matchup.py:54`. The V3.2 L3m coordinate is float32 and begins 400.178, 402.654, 405.127. Over the 140 bands between 396 and 704 nm the true center exceeds its label by a mean of 0.512 nm (range 0.023 to 0.996), and the labels step by 1, 2, or 3 nm where the true steps are 1.25 and 2.5 nm.
   - Size on Tchla, two measurements that should not be averaged:
     - Audit, 150 interior pixels: mean Tchla 0.457 mg m^-3 with the labels against 0.313 with the true centers, a ratio of means of 1.46. The shift is additive: labeled = 0.98 x true + 0.185 mg m^-3.
     - Envelope check, 2,000 scored pixels stratified by month and side: Tchla with the true centers is a median 0.715 of the labeled value (5th to 95th percentile 0.13 to 1.10), so labeled over true is 1.40 by the median pixel ratio.
   - Size on zero-clipped Tchla: 14 of 150 pixels with the labels against 42 of 150 with the true centers (audit); 6.9% against 22.6% of pixels, weighted (envelope check, 2,000 pixels).
   - Size on the other pigments, true over labeled, median pixel ratio (envelope check, 2,000 pixels): Chlc3 0.70, Fuco 0.71, Chlc12 0.71, Allo 0.71, HexFuco 0.76, Neo 0.76, ButFuco 0.78, Viola 0.78, MVchlb 0.80, Perid 0.85, Zea 0.86, DVchla 0.96. The audit's ratios of means on 150 pixels, labeled over true, are Chlc3 1.47, Chlc12 1.45, Fuco 1.43, HexFuco 1.36, Allo 1.55, Perid 1.21, Zea 1.20, DVchla 1.03.
   - The bias depends on water type, so it distorts contrasts and trends as well as levels. Labeled over true mean Tchla is 2.45 in the lowest `chlor_a` tercile and 1.24 in the highest (audit, 50 pixels each). Zea/Tchla reads 0.329 where the true-center value is 0.635 in the lowest tercile, and 0.071 against 0.077 in the highest. Fuco/Tchla barely moves (0.219 against 0.224).
   - The offset does not cancel against the background. The background runs through the same truncation, but the offset is additive, so `log_ratio_<pigment>` changes: adding 0.18 to a numerator of 0.1 and a denominator of 0.4 moves the ratio from 0.25 to 0.48 (audit, worked example).
   - The true centers are what the model was trained to see. On the 145 training spectra resampled at OCI band centers, true centers reproduce the trained means within 0.91 to 1.16, and integer labels give 1.23 to 1.34 for 11 of 13 pigments (audit). In the envelope check's version of the same test the median predicted over HPLC Tchla is 1.13 with true centers and 1.96 with integer labels. Whether true centers bring the pigments closer to HPLC in this region is untested.
   - Confidence: high on the defect and its direction, medium on the exact sizes.
   - Smallest fix: write float column names at `collocate_pace.py:151` and `background.py:123`, as the Level-2 reader already does with three decimals at `src/eddy_tracking/preprocess/pace.py:181-191`. `prediction.py:259-262` parses the name with `float(...)`, so it needs no change. Check any reader that selects an `Rrs_<int>` column by name.
   - Smallest test: rename the columns of one `silver/collocate_pace/<polarity>/eddy_<id>_rrs.parquet` to the file's float centers, run `run_sdp_on_pace_l3` on it, and compare with the stored pigments. Expect Tchla near 0.7 of the stored value and about three times as many zeros.
2. Negative predictions are clipped per pixel before the interior mean.
   - Evidence: `prediction.py:121-123` clips each pixel, and `src/eddy_tracking/pipeline/build_gold_table.py:43-46` averages with the zeros included.
   - Size: on 164,067 stored interior pixels the zero share is Tchla 4.8%, Chlc12 6.1%, Fuco 9.8%, Allo 10.9%, Viola 12.6%, MVchlb 14.9%, DVchla 20.8%, Neo 21.2% (audit, confirmed by running). The audit infers an upward bias in the interior mean of about 1% at 5% zeros and about 13% at 20% zeros, assuming the clipped values are symmetric scatter. The zeros concentrate on the Sargasso side in autumn, so the bias is selective. With true centers eight of the 13 pigments are clipped in 20.8% to 45.0% of pixels (2,000 pixels, weighted), so the bias grows after defect 1 is fixed (my inference from the two notes; not computed).
   - Confidence: medium. The direction is certain and the size is inferred.
   - Smallest fix: keep the unclipped median per pixel, average it over the interior, and clip the mean once. The model is linear after the inversion, so this is close to running SDP on the mean spectrum (audit, inferred). Keep `tchla_zero_frac` as the record of censoring.
   - Smallest test: for one eddy file, return the unclipped medians and compare the two interior means per composite.
3. `run_sdp` skips files that exist, and the pigment files on disk are older than the Rrs files.
   - Evidence: `src/eddy_tracking/pipeline/run_sdp.py:34-39` returns early when the output file exists. All `silver/collocate_pace` files were rewritten on 2026-10-13 and run past 2025-12-29. The `silver/pigments` files date from 2026-09-08 and 2026-09-09, 151 eddies have Rrs and no pigment file, and the new Rrs files hold 20 composite dates the old pigment files lack (audit, confirmed by running).
   - Status after the rebuild finished at about 21:05 on 2026-10-13: the audit saw the folder mid-rebuild. Checked afterwards, all 697 eddies with an Rrs file have a pigment file, 695 hold every date of their Rrs file, and 2 lack one date each (anticyclone 183 and cyclone 159). No continuing eddy was left with a truncated series, so this defect did not occur in tonight's run. The skip rule itself is unchanged.
   - Size today: none. Track ids and centers agree on all 520 shared dates in 91 eddies (audit), and all 813 older tracks keep their birth date (envelope check).
   - Size on a rerun that does not clear the folder: new eddies get a full series and continuing eddies stop at the old end date, which biases the late-age bins. `build_gold_table.py:151-157` joins by nearest date with no tolerance, so a stale file under a reused track id would join without an error.
   - Confidence: high on the mechanism.
   - Smallest fix: delete `silver/pigments` and the background outputs before the rerun that defect 1 requires.

## Limits of the method and of PACE Rrs here, largest effect first

1. On the Sargasso side SDP Tchla does not track chlorophyll.
   - Evidence, composite level against PACE `chlor_a` (measured, truncated wavelengths): south side median ratio 3.10, correlation of the logs 0.30, slope 0.32 (2,543 composites). In the two lowest chlorophyll bins over both sides (752 and 915 composites) SDP median Tchla is 0.21 and 0.24 mg m^-3 where `chlor_a` is 0.038 and 0.066. PACE `chlor_a` and Copernicus CHL agree with each other at a median ratio of 1.03 and correlation 0.985 (3,568 composites), though both are band-ratio products and a shared clear-water bias cannot be excluded.
   - Evidence that PACE spectra cause part of it: for south-side summer pixels (1,484 pixels, median inversion chlorophyll 0.044 mg m^-3) the model gives training spectra of the same chlorophyll 0.028 mg m^-3 and gives PACE 0.256. By band the excess is +0.127 from 670 to 699 nm, +0.094 from 480 to 520 nm, +0.078 from 588 to 613 nm (where OCI has no Rrs bands and the spline bridges 588.086 to 612.730 nm), and -0.096 from 401 to 440 nm (measured).
   - Evidence that the model causes part of it: applied to its own 145 training spectra, the stored model clips Tchla to zero for 10.3% of them, and for 20.9% of the 67 with HPLC Tchla under 0.1 mg m^-3 (envelope check). The slope of predicted on measured Tchla is 0.67 in-sample (audit). The paper's cross-validated absolute error for Tchla is 0.498 of the mean, about 0.15 mg m^-3, against 0.03 to 0.1 mg m^-3 present on the Sargasso side (regional note, inferred from Table 2). Prochaska and Frouin (2025) report a low-chlorophyll PACE-like example in which phytoplankton absorption is not detected at 3 sigma (regional note, read through a web fetch).
   - Share owed to defect 1: a factor of about 1.4. The envelope check estimates that the overall median ratio to `chlor_a` would fall from 2.3 to about 1.7 after the fix, by multiplying and not by measuring on composites. The audit's 150 pixels give 1.94 on the mean with labels and 1.33 with true centers.
   - Confidence: high.
   - Design: take chlorophyll on the Sargasso side from Copernicus CHL or `chlor_a`, and do not divide any pigment by SDP Tchla there.
   - Smallest test: after the rerun, repeat the composite comparison of `eddy_mean_Tchla` with `silver/pace_chl/eddy_chlor_a.parquet` and with `CHL` in `gold/eddy_plankton_table.parquet`, by month and side.
2. A seasonal feature at 687 to 688 nm in PACE Rrs drives Tchla through the year.
   - Evidence: the mean PACE second derivative at 688 nm, in training standard deviations, swings from -7.2 in July to +5.7 in December on the south side and from -4.7 in July to +19.3 in December on the north side (measured, 380 to 500 pixels per month and side). The 670 to 699 nm band is 10% of the wavelengths and carries 24.4% of the Tchla coefficient weight.
   - Size: among south-side pixels with `chlor_a` of 0.05 to 0.10 mg m^-3, June to August (549 pixels) has 1.6% zeros and median Tchla 0.281 mg m^-3, and October to December (615 pixels) has 40.0% zeros and median Tchla 0.034, while the inversion chlorophyll is 0.058 in both. The difference in the mean second derivative times the mean Tchla coefficients is -0.257 mg m^-3, of which -0.168 (65%) comes from 670 to 699 nm (measured). The median ratio of SDP Tchla to `chlor_a` falls from 4.56 in July (468 composites) to 0.62 in December (146 composites).
   - Cause: untested. The envelope check infers a residual of the oxygen B band (687 nm) in the atmospheric correction that grows with the path length through the air, with a possible contribution from chlorophyll fluorescence near 683 nm. The data-quality note supports the first reading from separate evidence: NASA lists residual oxygen and water-vapor artifacts in the red as a known issue in V3.2 (PACE OCI V3 processing notes, April 2026), and the V3.2 vicarious gains jump by about 2% across 685 to 692 nm (measured from one L2 file header).
   - Consequence for eddy results: at fixed `chlor_a`, Tchla falls from summer to autumn, so an eddy followed across those months would show falling Tchla and rising pigment-to-Tchla ratios even if its water did not change. An age trend and this seasonal artifact cannot be told apart in the present outputs (my inference). The close median agreement with `chlor_a` from November to January is two errors of opposite sign (envelope check, inferred).
   - `keep_valid_sdp_rows` (`src/eddy_tracking/validation/quality.py:104-122`, uncommitted) removes 104 of 3,571 composites for zero Tchla, 103 of them on the south side between September and February. Those composites hold 28.5% of the zero-Tchla pixels, so most zeros stay in the rows that pass. The rule was tuned on truncated values and will remove more rows after the fix (audit, inferred).
   - Confidence: high on the feature and its size, low on the cause.
   - Smallest test: on the 11,563 scored pixels, recompute Tchla with the second derivative from 680 to 699 nm set to the training mean, and check whether the monthly ratio to `chlor_a` on the south side flattens.
3. Most accessory pigments restate Tchla, and the ratios restate its denominator.
   - Evidence: Kramer et al. (2022) contains no Tchla-only baseline. In the training data, measured Tchla alone explains as much variance as cross-validated SDP, or more, for 9 of 12 accessory pigments; only Perid, Zea, and DVchla do better through SDP (method note, computed; the baseline is in-sample). In the 332-cast PVST run SDP most likely loses to a chlorophyll law on 11 of 12 pigments (project memory).
   - Evidence in the pipeline outputs: the 11 accessory pigments other than DVchla sum to more than 1.64 times a non-zero Tchla, the largest value in the 145 HPLC samples, in 8.26% of interior pixels, and DVchla exceeds Tchla in 8.40% (measured, 1,249,453 pixels). The model does the same to its own training spectra: DVchla at or above Tchla for 22 of 145 (audit).
   - Confidence: high that this limits interpretation.
   - Design: report an accessory pigment only after showing what it adds beyond Copernicus CHL on the same composites.
4. North of the axis from November to March the spectra leave the training range, and the outputs do not show it.
   - Evidence: the shares in the section above. On the north side from December to February 63% of scored pixels are outside the training 95th percentile (1,268 pixels), yet Tchla is clipped in only 8.4% of those, so the zero-Tchla rule does not catch them. In the 372 scored pixels where the inversion chlorophyll collapses (371 on the north side), `chlor_a` is a median 0.69 mg m^-3 and the fitted absorption by colored dissolved matter is a median 0.133 m^-1 against 0.015 for the whole sample (measured).
   - Size: few rows. The north side has 51 composites from December to February in the `chlor_a` comparison.
   - Confidence: high.
   - Design: flag or drop north-side composites from November to March by month and side.
5. Single pixels and thin rings sit near the noise level.
   - Evidence: in one L2 granule (2025-04-04, 600 clear-water boxes of 25 pixels) the single-pixel scatter of the second difference is 4e-6 to 1e-5 sr^-1 nm^-2 against a scene-to-scene spread of 1e-6 to 4e-6, and the data-quality note infers a signal-to-noise ratio of about 1 to 2 for an 8-day bin in clear water (moderate confidence on the order of magnitude). The L3 mapped file carries no observation count, so a bin built from 1 pixel looks like a bin built from 60. Across the 100 coefficient sets, the interquartile range relative to the median is 0.53 for Tchla and about 1.0 for Fuco, Chlc12, Allo, and Neo on the training spectra themselves (audit).
   - Consequence: interior means over many pixels are what this evidence supports. The 0.2 R rings with a 3-pixel gate rest on per-pixel values of this quality; the audit did not check those notebook cells.
   - Confidence: medium.

## What the truncation does to the earlier validation numbers

- Every pigment the L3 pipeline has written carries the shift. The truncating line is in the first commit of the repository (2026-04-01; I checked the git history for this report). That includes the 901 eddy-day results in project memory: median `chlor_a` over SDP Tchla of 0.50, and SDP pigments tracking SDP Tchla at r^2 0.68 to 0.98.
- `sdp-validation/sdpval/matchup.py:54` builds `Rrs_{int(wl)}` columns from an L3 pixel window, so every SDP-against-HPLC number produced through that reader used shifted wavelengths. The audit states this for the BATS matchups in `sdp-validation` and labels it an inference.
- The 2026-07-28 runs in project memory read Level-2 granules, and their status is different. Those are the 11 BATS boxes and the 332-cast PVST run (344 Tchla pairs, log-log slope 0.721, median bias +13.6%, Zea log r -0.196, the 11-of-12 chlorophyll-law result). The audit confirms that the current Level-2 path keeps float wavelengths (`preprocess/pace.py:181-191`, `prediction.py:154,197-200`), and I checked that the reader already wrote three-decimal names at commit 11cbd33 (2026-07-26). The scripts for those runs are deleted, so nobody can confirm which wavelengths reached the spline; the audit says only that it cannot be checked. Most likely those numbers do not carry the shift (my inference).
- If the PVST numbers are free of the shift, the range compression, the backwards Zea, and the loss to a chlorophyll law are results about the method, and fixing the truncation will not improve them.
- A Level-2 against Level-3 comparison will show a spurious offset of the truncation's size until the L3 path is fixed (audit).
- After the fix, expect lower Tchla everywhere, about three times as many zero-clipped Tchla pixels (22.6% against 6.9%, 2,000 pixels), and more rows failing `keep_valid_sdp_rows`, nearly all on the Sargasso side in autumn. The present 7% zero share is partly a product of the truncation (envelope check, inferred). The stored model also clips 10.3% of its own training spectra, so the zeros that appear are the method's behavior at low chlorophyll.

## Checked and found not to matter

- SST and SSS: a -10 C change in SST moves the median pixel by at most 0.39% for any pigment (audit, 71 pixels). The only effect is that pixels without MODIS SST are dropped, 3.3% of sampled interior pixels and about 10% in January and February.
- Paper equations against code: the printed adg slope sign and the band in the bbp exponent differ from the authors' MATLAB. The repository follows the MATLAB, which the coefficients were fitted with.
- Preprocessing, residual, second difference, ensemble median, latitude order, composite dates, units, and product version: all match the paper and the authors' code. A rerun of 11,563 pixels reproduces the stored pigments within 0.0001 mg m^-3, and all 103 PACE files are V3.2.
- The inversion: 0 of 11,563 pixels failed to converge, and its chlorophyll is within a factor of 2 of `chlor_a` for 91% of pixels.
- OCI band spacing and bandwidth as such: the 145 training spectra resampled to OCI bands, gap included, stay inside the envelope and lose about 0.1 in R^2.
- Running SDP on an 8-day composite: every band of a bin averages the same pixels, and for a clipped, noisy retrieval the composite-first order is the less biased one.
- Aerosol-correction error and random noise in the blue: pixel deviations correlate at 0.97 to 0.99 between adjacent bands and mostly cancel in a second difference (one L2 granule).
- Negative Rrs: 1.3% of scored pixels (envelope check) and 2.6% of valid pixels in one composite (audit).
- Coverage gate applied before SDP drops pixels: 31 of 873 eddy-composites (3.6%) end below 0.8 true coverage.
- Eddy polarity: Tchla is clipped in 7.8% of anticyclone and 6.9% of cyclone interior pixels.
- CDOM slope, whitecaps, sun glint, Trichodesmium, coccolithophore blooms: the slope SDP assumes (0.0148 to 0.0171 nm^-1) sits inside the 0.013 to 0.021 nm^-1 measured on NAAMES (Allen et al. 2020), and the rest are spectrally smooth or rare in open water here (regional note, partly recalled).
- Coefficient provenance: the stored coefficients fit the 145 training spectra (in-sample R^2 0.81 for Tchla), but `scripts/train_sdp.py` does not reproduce them today. The audit reads the archive README as a Python refit; the envelope check infers another run or random number generator, possibly the MATLAB code. No reported number changes. In the methods, describe them as SDP coefficients fitted to the Kramer et al. (2021) data and do not call them NASA's.
